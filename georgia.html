📝 Synopsis
Columbia Co. — Clear Winner
For a teacher + remote worker household, Columbia County is the safest, most affordable high-QOL option. Grovetown for value, Evans for premium suburban life, Martinez for the middle. A+ schools, very low crime, housing 30–60% below US median — a combination Fulton simply cannot match.
Fulton Co. — The Long Game
The only county where income trajectory steepens over time. Atlanta's media market turns a Head of Video title into an $80–110K credential. But the cost premium is real and schools only deliver in North Fulton. Trading affordability for career ceiling.
Athens — Best Culture Play
UGA gives Athens restaurant density, live music, and walkability that rival cities twice its size. Compelling for a teacher who values urban lifestyle at sub-Atlanta pricing. Career ceiling outside academia or healthcare hits around year 6–7.
Augusta City — Avoid Unless Tethered
Richmond County city proper grades C+ for good reasons. The smart play if your life requires the Augusta metro: live in Columbia County, commute in — A+ safety and schools while accessing Augusta's healthcare and military jobs.
