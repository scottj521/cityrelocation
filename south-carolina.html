A
Coastal · Historic · Pop. 430K

Wide internal variance: Mount Pleasant is pure A+, downtown expensive, North Charleston pulls the county down with crime. Families should anchor to Mount Pleasant or Summerville.

📝 Synopsis
Greenville Co. ≈ Fulton Co. (GA)
Both are the dominant economic powerhouses of their states. Greenville is significantly more affordable than Atlanta but the career ceiling is lower. For a remote worker Greenville wins on QOL-per-dollar; for career trajectory, Fulton/Atlanta still leads.
York Co. ≈ Columbia Co. (GA)
Same formula: live in SC, commute into a bigger metro. York is stronger because Charlotte's job market dwarfs Augusta's. Tega Cay and Fort Mill are the Evans/Martinez equivalents — safe, A+ schools, dollar goes further than the major city.
Lexington Co. — SC's Teacher Sweet Spot
Irmo and Lexington town are SC's Grovetown/Evans. Crime A− (safer than 86% of US cities), excellent schools to teach in, housing costs that fit a dual-income household. The teacher + remote worker formula works best here in SC.
Charleston Co. — Prestige With a Price
Mount Pleasant is SC's Alpharetta — the premium suburb that earns its price. But Charleston's market is even more expensive and the job market is narrowly tied to aerospace and tourism. Unless you're well compensated there, Summerville is the smarter entry point.
